Cross-Border Rosetta Stone

Translating US & UK Insurance Jargon

Moving between the United States and the United Kingdom? Don't let differing policy vocabularies leave you underinsured or paying out of pocket.

Insurance Concept 🇺🇸 United States Standard 🇬🇧 United Kingdom Standard
First-Dollar Out-of-Pocket Expense Deductible (e.g. $1,500 – $7,500 per year before carrier coverage starts). Policy Excess (Compulsory + Voluntary, typically £100 – £500 per claim).
Fixed Per-Visit Fee Copay ($20–$50 per doctor visit, $150–$300 ER visit). Zero copay at point of NHS service; nominal excess on private PMI outpatient appointments.
Routine Healthcare Foundation ACA Marketplace / Employer Group Health Plan + High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA. National Health Service (NHS) funded via general taxation + Supplemental Private Medical Insurance (PMI).
Death Benefit Taxation Generally income tax-free under IRC § 101(a); subject to federal estate tax only above high exemptions ($13.6M+). Income tax-free; however, unless written in trust, the lump sum is added to the estate and subject to 40% Inheritance Tax (IHT).
Homeowner Dwelling Coverage HO-3 / HO-5 Homeowners Policy with separate NFIP flood insurance rider. Buildings & Contents Insurance with Flood Re integration and subsidence protection.
