About InsuranceBuddy

Bridging the Financial Atlantic with Transparent Advice

We founded InsuranceBuddy to eliminate predatory commission incentives, decipher dense policy clauses, and give households in the US and UK total clarity when buying insurance.

Our Core Principles

Insurance is the single largest financial defensive shield any family or business will ever purchase. Yet, the industry thrives on confusing terminology: deductibles versus excess, open perils versus named perils, and term insurance versus predatory whole-life cash value constructs.

🎯 Radical Editorial Independence

We refuse to allow insurance carriers to buy editorial ratings or alter our rankings. When whole life is mathematically poor value, we say so plainly. When an NHS wait list warrants private PMI, we provide verified wait time statistics.

🌐 Cross-Border Actuarial Rigor

Our dual-jurisdiction team holds verified credentials in both the United States and the United Kingdom, ensuring our guidance adheres strictly to the regulations of state insurance departments and the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA).
